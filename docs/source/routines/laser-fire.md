# Laser fire

**Address** `$33F9-$3435` · **Group** Combat

Checks that no bolt is in flight (`$036E`), heat is below `$B0` and the laser is enabled (`$0370`), then checks the fire input. Firing sets the bolt timer to `$80`, latches provocation in `$03BF`, requests the shot sound and adds `$0C` to the heat at `$0347`, capped at `$BF`. The target is tested later when the bolt finishes.
